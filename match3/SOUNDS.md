# Sounds

All sound effects are real recordings released as CC0 (public domain) by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets).
They were trimmed, leveled, converted to 32 kHz mono WAV and embedded in `index.html` as data URIs.

- Interface Sounds: `click_005`, `click_002`, `glass_004`, `glass_005`, `maximize_004`, `confirmation_002`, `minimize_001`
- Impact Sounds: `impactGlass_light_000–004`, `impactGlass_heavy_001/003`, `impactMining_000–004`, `impactSoft_medium_000–002`
- Casino Audio: `card-slide-1..3`, `chips-collide-1/3/4`, `chip-lay-1/2`
